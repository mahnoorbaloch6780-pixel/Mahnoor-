Our Heritage & Soil

Honoring the Ancient
Art of Chai

Chai Wala began in the rolling, cloud-draped slopes of high-altitude Himalayan hills. We grew tired of commercially crushed dust bags steeped in industrial shortcuts.

Working directly alongside fourth-generation tea masters and organic spice agroforesters, we curate harvests that honor slow, meditative brewing traditions. Every blend is hand-tumbled in small wooden vats with cracked spices so oils are released precisely when warm steam touches your mug.

“A cup of chai is not just tea; it is warmth, hospitality, and a quiet pause in a rushing world.”

— Kabir Mehta, Founder & Chai Wala
