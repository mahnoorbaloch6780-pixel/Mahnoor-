## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the tactile richness, warmth, and slow-living atmosphere of artisanal tea rituals. The aesthetic marries editorial elegance with inviting hospitality. It targets discerning tea enthusiasts and wellness seekers who value craft, slow living, and organic origins.

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Nuances**:
- Generous, unhurried negative space that conveys quiet luxury.
- Soft, organic contours that evoke smooth river stones, handcrafted ceramics, and hand-rolled tea leaves.
- High typographic contrast between literary display serifs and clear, contemporary sans serifs.
- Grounded warmth through tinted backgrounds and warm ambient light, rejecting sterile digital whites and cold gray shadows.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from raw botanicals, steeped spices, and natural ceramics:

- **Primary (`#5C3A21` - Spiced Bark):** Grounded, deep roasted brown. Used for principal text, high-emphasis branding elements, solid primary CTA buttons, and framing contours.
- **Secondary (`#D4A24C` - Wild Honey):** Warm golden amber. Serves as a luminous accent for key badges, active states, focus rings, rating stars, and celebratory micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#4F7942` - Fresh Leaf Green):** A fresh botanical green. Applied to organic certifications, harvest notes, availability indicators, and sustainability callouts.
- **Neutral Base (`#FFF6E9` - Steamed Cream):** The foundational canvas color. Provides a warm, comforting parchment undertone across all primary screens.
- **Surface Variant (`#F7EAD6` - Clay Hearth):** A slightly deeper cream used for card containers, subtle borders, input backgrounds, and visual layering without relying on harsh drop shadows.
- **Muted Foreground (`#8C6E54` - Steeped Tan):** Used for secondary typography, metadata labels, placeholders, and inactive outlines.

Pure black (`#000000`) and clinical digital white (`#FFFFFF`) are strictly avoided across all interface views.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative editorial storytelling with effortless functional readability:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Playfair Display**. It provides high editorial character, sharp transition serifs, and literary warmth. Used exclusively for product titles, story headers, hero narratives, and brand quotes.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Chosen for its friendly terminal curves and open counters, ensuring high legibility across dense ingredient listings, brewing directions, prices, and commerce flows.
- **Case Formatting:** Use uppercase sparingly, restricted to `label-sm` tracking tags (e.g., origin badges like "SINGLE ESTATE" or "HARVEST 2024") paired with wide letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an expansive, breathable grid that mimics art-book publishing:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop screens with a max-width container of 1320px; 8-column layout on tablet screens (768px - 1024px); 4-column layout on mobile devices (< 768px).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm is prioritized over data density. Key content modules are allowed to breathe with `space-xl` and `space-2xl` block spacing to preserve the slow-living brand ambiance.
- **Breaks & Offsets:** Product listings adopt asymmetrical alternating layouts where imagery overlaps with container borders to reflect organic curation rather than rigid e-commerce catalogs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm ambient depth and tactile tonal nesting rather than clinical shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: Steamed Cream (`#FFF6E9`).
  - Elevated Container / Cards: Warm Ceramic (`#FDFBF7`) or Clay Hearth (`#F7EAD6`).
  - Overlays / Modals: High-purity Porcelain (`#FFFDF9`) framed by soft tinted borders.
- **Ambient Shadow Crafting:** Shadows are tinted with pulverized tea-leaf brown (`#5C3A21`) rather than black or neutral gray:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.04)`.
  - *Interactive Hover / Floating:* `0px 20px 36px -8px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.10), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.05)`.
- **Low-Contrast Boundaries:** Surface distinctions are accented by subtle 1px outlines colored with 8% to 12% opacity of the primary brown (`rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature inspired by ceramic vessels and steeping teaware:

- **Base Radius:** Interactive controls like buttons, text fields, and pills utilize pill-shaped roundness (`roundedness: 3` / full curvature).
- **Card Structures:** Content cards and modules adopt generous radii of 24px (`rounded-2xl`) to 32px (`rounded-3xl`), creating a soft, approachable visual boundary.
- **Image Treatments:** Product and botanical photography features matching heavy-rounded frames or organic arch masks to reinforce the artisanal aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#5C3A21` with high-contrast `#FFF6E9` text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `1rem 2rem`. On hover, transitions to a soft deep roast tone (`#472B17`) with a warm ambient lift.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with a 1.5px solid border in `#5C3A21`, colored in `#5C3A21`. Hover fills with a warm tint (`rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.05)`).
- **Tertiary / Botanical Action:** Filled in fresh leaf green (`#4F7942`) with `#FFF6E9` text, reserved for tea subscriptions, eco-friendly add-ons, or harvest discovery paths.

### Chips & Badges
- Fully pill-shaped with minimal height (`28px` - `32px`).
- Flavor profile chips (e.g., "Cardamom", "Smoked Saffron") use a `#F7EAD6` surface, `#5C3A21` text, and no border.
- Harvest and organic badges use a pale leaf tint (`rgba(79, 121, 66, 0.12)`) paired with `#4F7942` text.

### Cards
- **Product & Story Cards:** Framed with `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl` corners, resting on a `#FDFBF7` surface with tinted amber-brown ambient drop shadows.
- Generous internal padding of `space-lg` (1.75rem).
- Card footers feature clear pricing separated by soft horizontal dividers tinted with `rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.08)`.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- Text inputs use pill or heavy rounded-xl geometry, a soft background of `#F7EAD6`, and 1px border of `rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.12)`.
- Focus states swap borders for a warm golden ring (`#D4A24C`) with `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 162, 76, 0.25)`.
- Labels are positioned outside inputs in `label-md` weight.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radios and checkboxes feature smooth rounded geometries (checkboxes use a gentle `6px` radius).
- Selected state fills with `#5C3A21` displaying an organic check icon in `#FFF6E9`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Steep Guide Tracker:** Visual sensory bar indicating steeping time, ideal water temperature, and aroma intensity, styled with honey (`#D4A24C`) and leaf green (`#4F7942`) gradient tracks.
- **Tasting Notes Wheel:** Circular flavor breakdown with tinted tactile segments and serif central identifiers.
